What the Wader needs

    • Quiet room, phone a hand's width from your mouth and a little to the side.
    • Leave half a second between clicks or takes.
    • Mix tongue clicks, throat clicks and a low croaky rattle.
    • Lots of rough takes beat a few careful ones.

    The Wader preview is close to what the game will get: pitched down, thickened, with a pool hall's echo.
