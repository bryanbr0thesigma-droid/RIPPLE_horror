# Branding credits

Every image in `game/assets/branding/` is drawn by the scripts in this folder: shapes, text, flat
illustrations and textures are code. Nothing was made by an AI image model. The only existing
pictures used are below. All of them are public domain or CC0 except the Wader, which needs a
credit line (CC BY 4.0).

|---|---|---|---|---|

## Made-up names

Pinewater Field Station, DEEP END soda, HIGH & DRY coffee, VACANCY magazine and TELLURIC electronics
are invented for the game. None of them copies a real brand's logo, colours or packaging.
