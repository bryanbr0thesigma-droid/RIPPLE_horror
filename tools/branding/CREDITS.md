# Branding credits

Every image in `game/assets/branding/` is drawn by the scripts in this folder: shapes, text, flat
illustrations and textures are code. Nothing was made by an AI image model. The only existing
pictures used are below. All of them are public domain or CC0 except the Wader, which needs a
credit line (CC BY 4.0).

|---|---|---|---|---|

These come from `../falcon_tape/assets` (see `../falcon_tape/assets/CREDITS.md`).

The magazine cover photos (`covers/`) are stills rendered in the Godot engine. One is the game's own
Shallow Halls, with the Wader standing small in a doorway (so the CC BY credit above applies to
it). The other two are homages to the Backrooms' Level 0 and Level Run. They were built in the
engine from the game's own textures, plus a wallpaper and ceiling-tile texture drawn by script. No
photograph or artwork from the Backrooms is used.

## Knock-off brands

Chlora-Cola, Floater's, Liminal Geographic and SORNY are parodies made up for the game. Their names
and colour schemes nod at real brands, but nothing is copied: the lettering, logos and artwork are
drawn by these scripts (in the open fonts above), and none of them uses a real brand's logo,
typeface, mascot or artwork. Pinewater Field Station is invented.
