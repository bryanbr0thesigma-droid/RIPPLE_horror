RIPPLE - ART AND BRANDING REQUESTS
==================================

Everything below is art I can drop straight into the game. Nothing here is required to play;
right now each spot shows plain placeholder text or a blank surface. Items are grouped by where
they show up, then by priority. The fake companies the art needs are listed in PART 1.


HOW TO DELIVER
--------------

- Format: PNG. Use the exact pixel sizes given (they are powers of two or simple ratios so the
  headset handles them well). Bigger is fine if it is the same shape; I will scale it down.
- Transparency: only where it says "transparent background". Everything else should be a solid,
  full-bleed image (no transparent edges).
- File names: use the names given in [brackets], so I know what goes where.
- Where to put them: send them in the chat, or commit them to  game/assets/branding/  on the
  claude/admiring-ride-o5042e branch (or any branch) and tell me.
- Project rule: no AI-generated art. Make it yourself, or build it from CC0 / CC BY sources
  (tell me the sources for CC BY so I can credit them).
- Readability in VR: text on a sign of about 1 m wide should be at least about 1/12 of the
  image height to be readable from a couple of metres. Use high contrast. Small print is fine as
  decoration, but nobody will be able to read it.
- Era and style: late 80s to mid 90s, slightly worn, government-issue. Think photocopied
  pamphlets, enamel signs, felt letter boards, laminated notices.
